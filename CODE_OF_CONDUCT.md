# Code of conduct

tabsight is a welcoming project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else, is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, including with beginners.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language and unwanted personal attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who repeatedly break these rules.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the address in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled in confidence.
